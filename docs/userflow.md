### Summary of Modules
1. **RescueFlow Emergency Command Center**: Coordinates requests across hospitals, blood banks, and donors.
2. **Parallel Source Matching**: Concurrently queries verified blood banks for confirmed/unverified stock and consented donors within radius.
3. **Donor Availability & Request Inbox**: Clear Accept / Decline actions with travel guidance and no public disclosure of identity.
4. **QR Donation Pass & Scanner**: Opaque, short-lived token linking arrival to active request; verified by authenticated staff.
5. **Clinical Screening & Donation Event**: Medical deferral or eligibility outcome recorded by facility staff; stock reconciliation decoupled from donor lead acceptance.
6. **Live Network Map**: Public view of verified facilities & camps; privacy-protected aggregate donor coverage for coordinators.
7. **BloodBuddy Awareness Chatbot**: Multilingual Q&A on donation myths/facts with medical safety boundaries.
8. **Donation Camps Portal**: NGO organizer camp publishing, capacity limits, and QR check-in.
